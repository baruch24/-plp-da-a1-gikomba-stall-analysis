Wanjiku's problem is that Wanjiku runs a fresh-produce stall at Gikomba Market. For the last two weeks she has recorded her daily sales in a notebook and kept a rough record of her stock, but she has no clear picture of how the business is doing. She hired me as a freelance analyst to answer four questions: how is the business doing, is it growing, what should she restock, and where should she focus her effort? I analysed her 14 days of sales and her stock record using core Python only (no pandas or NumPy) and finished with practical recommendations.
My top three findings are that:
Weekends are by far her best days: Saturday and Sunday average KES 4,900 a day against KES 2,685 on weekdays, which is 82.5% higher.
The business is growing: sales rose 10.7% from KES 22,050 in week 1 to KES 24,400 in week 2, with total sales of KES 46,450 over the 14 days.
Two products need restocking now: Sukuma (9 kg against a reorder level of 15 kg) and Tomatoes (18 kg against 25 kg). Avocado has the best gross margin at 42.9%.
